# ATLAS — Data Dictionary

**Project:** Corporate Governance, Business Strategy & Financial Intelligence  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Define the variables, measurements, calculations, and sources used throughout the ATLAS research project.

## 1. Financial Variables

| Variable | Definition | Calculation / Measurement | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Total revenue generated during a fiscal year | Reported revenue | USD |
| Revenue Growth | Annual change in revenue | ((Current Revenue - Previous Revenue) / Previous Revenue) × 100 | % |
| Operating Income | Income generated from normal business operations | Reported operating income | USD |
| Operating Margin | Operating income relative to revenue | (Operating Income / Revenue) × 100 | % |
| Total Assets | Total recorded company assets | Reported total assets | USD |
| Total Liabilities | Total recorded company liabilities | Reported total liabilities | USD |
| Operating Cash Flow | Cash generated by operating activities | Reported operating cash flow | USD |
| Return on Assets (ROA) | Profitability relative to assets | (Net Income / Average Total Assets) × 100 | % |
| Debt-to-Assets | Proportion of assets financed by debt | Interest-Bearing Debt / Total Assets | % |
| Capital Expenditure | Spending on long-term physical assets | Reported capital expenditures | USD |
| R&D Intensity | Research and development spending relative to revenue | (R&D Expense / Revenue) × 100 | % |

**Important financial definitions:**
- Average Total Assets = (Beginning Total Assets + Ending Total Assets) / 2.
- Interest-bearing debt includes short-term borrowings and long-term debt, where separately reported.
- Financial statement values will be collected using consistent units and reporting conventions.
- A missing or undisclosed value will be marked as missing rather than assumed to be zero.

## 2. Corporate Governance and Legal Variables

| Variable | Definition | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Board Size | Number of directors serving on the board | Number of directors |
| Board Independence | Proportion of directors classified as independent | Independent Directors / Total Directors × 100 |
| CEO-Chair Separation | Whether the CEO and board chair positions are held by different individuals | Yes / No / Other structure |
| Executive Compensation | Compensation disclosed for named executive officers | USD; report relevant compensation categories |
| Shareholder Voting Structure | Voting rights and share-class arrangements | Qualitative classification |
| Material Litigation | Material litigation disclosed in corporate filings | Category, status, and description |
| Regulatory Matters | Material regulatory investigations, proceedings, or enforcement matters | Category, status, and description |
| Governance Policies | Disclosed corporate governance policies and practices | Documented qualitative indicators |

**Legal and governance coding rules:**
- Record the fiscal year or reporting date associated with each observation.
- Distinguish allegations, investigations, settlements, and final legal findings.
- Do not assume that the existence of litigation establishes wrongdoing.
- Record the source document and relevant filing section.
- Use consistent coding definitions across all companies.

## 3. Business Strategy Variables

| Variable | Definition | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue Segments | Major business divisions generating revenue | Segment names and revenue |
| Revenue Concentration | Dependence on major business segments | Largest segment revenue / Total Revenue × 100 |
| R&D Investment | Spending on research and development | USD and R&D intensity |
| Capital Allocation | How the company distributes financial resources | Qualitative analysis of investment, acquisitions, dividends, and repurchases |
| Strategic Initiatives | Major announced business strategies | Documented qualitative analysis |
| Geographic Diversification | Distribution of business activity across geographic markets | Reported geographic revenue |
| Competitive Positioning | How the company describes its position relative to competitors | Qualitative analysis supported by company disclosures |

## 4. Industry-Specific Variables

Some financial indicators are not directly comparable across industries. ATLAS will therefore collect additional industry-specific measures.

| Industry | Additional measures |
|---|---|
| Technology | R&D intensity, cloud or product segment growth where applicable |
| Banking | Net interest margin, efficiency ratio, regulatory capital ratios |
| Retail | Comparable-store sales growth where disclosed, inventory turnover |
| Energy | Production volumes, capital expenditure, operating cash flow |
| Pharmaceuticals | R&D intensity, product revenue concentration, pipeline disclosures |

Industry-specific metrics will be analyzed separately rather than combined into a single universal financial score.

## 5. Data Source Hierarchy

ATLAS will prioritize sources in the following order:

1. SEC annual reports (10-K) and official company filings.
2. SEC proxy statements (DEF 14A).
3. SEC Company Facts API and XBRL financial data.
4. Official company investor-relations publications.
5. Official regulatory and government publications.

Secondary sources may provide context but will not replace primary evidence when primary disclosures are available.

## 6. Data Recording Standards

Every collected observation should include:

- Company name.
- Industry.
- Fiscal year.
- Variable name.
- Reported value.
- Unit of measurement.
- Source document.
- SEC filing accession number or source reference, where applicable.
- Relevant page or filing section.
- Retrieval date.
- Notes about adjustments, missing values, or unusual reporting conventions.

## 7. Data Quality and Comparability

Before analysis:

1. Verify reported figures against original company filings.
2. Check units, currencies, and fiscal-year dates.
3. Avoid mixing quarterly and annual financial figures.
4. Document accounting or classification differences.
5. Flag missing or inconsistent observations.
6. Use industry-specific interpretation where financial measures are not directly comparable.

## 8. Research Integrity

All variables must be defined before their results are interpreted.

ATLAS will distinguish:
- Directly reported company information.
- Calculated financial indicators.
- Researcher-assigned qualitative classifications.
- Researcher interpretations and conclusions.

Any composite scores developed later will have documented criteria, transparent weighting, and limitations.
